# Risk Register & Active Mitigation Log (`RISK_REGISTER.md`)

**Project:** SarcasTone — A Leakage-Aware Comparative Study of Textual Semantics and Speech Prosody for Multimodal Sarcasm Detection  

| Risk ID | Risk Category | Description of Risk | Severity | Status | Implemented Mitigation & Verification |
| :---: | :--- | :--- | :---: | :---: | :--- |
| **R-01** | **Dataset Access / Media Completeness** | MUStARD++ (`1,202` rows) hosts its videos as unarchived files on Google Drive, which blocks automated bulk downloads and lacks 514 clips locally. | High | **RESOLVED (Gate 0)** | Formally activated the authorized Gate 0 fallback to **Original MUStARD ($N=690$)**, where 100% of `.mp4` target and context clips are locally present and verified, while linking MUStARD++ subgroup annotations (`Sarcasm_Type`, `Valence`, `Arousal`, `Emotion`) for the 688/690 overlapping scenes. |
| **R-02** | **MP4 Audio Decoding Without System FFmpeg** | Local Windows environment lacks `ffmpeg.exe` on `PATH`, and `torchaudio.load()` fails without `torchcodec`. | High | **RESOLVED (Phase 0)** | Installed `av` (PyAV `19.0.2`), `soundfile` (`0.14.0`), `librosa` (`1.0.0`), `praat-parselmouth` (`0.4.7`), and `opensmile` (`2.6.0`). Verified direct decoding and 16 kHz mono resampling from `.mp4`. |
| **R-03** | **Scene & Dialogue Context Leakage** | Adjacent clips from the same sitcom scene share verbatim context dialogue turns (62 target utterances appear inside another clip's context). Random splits leak scenes across train/test. | High | **MITIGATED (Phase 2–3)** | Constructed a Connected-Component Scene Graph combining verbatim turn sharing ($\ge 20$ chars) and contiguous episode clip numbering, isolating disjoint scene clusters for Scene-Grouped 5-Fold CV (`Protocol B`) with automated zero-overlap assertions. |
| **R-04** | **Speaker & Show Label Confounding** | Strong base-rate skew across speakers (`DOROTHY` 97.4% sarcastic, `CHANDLER` 75.9% vs. `JOEY` 8.3%, `ROSS` 13.2%) and shows (`GOLDENGIRLS` 97.5%, `SARCASMOHOLICS` 100%). Models may memorize speaker voiceprint or vocabulary instead of sarcasm cues. | High | **MITIGATED (Phase 7–8)** | Implemented (1) **Speaker-Only Diagnostic Baseline** to quantify the exact ceiling of speaker-ID bias, (2) **Leave-One-Speaker-Out (LOSO)** and **Speaker-Grouped CV** (`Protocol C`), and (3) strict exclusion of speaker/show IDs from text and audio model inputs. |
| **R-05** | **Unreliable $F_0$ / Very Short Clips / Sitcom Laugh Tracks** | Target clips range down to `0.38 s` (`2_540.mp4`), and sitcoms include studio audience laugh tracks that distort pitch and energy. | Medium | **MITIGATED (Phase 5)** | Added energy/periodicity speech gating, voiced-frame ratio tracking (`voiced_fraction`), explicit quality flags (`f0_unvoiced_flag`, `short_clip_flag`), and strictly fold-internal median imputation so unvoiced clips never leak global statistics or crash classifiers. |
| **R-06** | **Overfitting on Small Sample Size ($N=690$)** | High-dimensional embeddings (768-d `roberta-base` + 768-d `wav2vec2-base` + prosody) can overfit $N=690$ examples. | Medium | **MITIGATED (Phase 7)** | Used frozen pretrained encoders, inside-fold `StandardScaler`, $L_2$-regularized `LogisticRegression` / linear models tuned via inner cross-validation, and feature-group ablations. |
| **R-07** | **Underpowered Subgroup / Disagreement Slices** | Some sarcasm types (e.g., `LIK` with $N=3$) or strict disagreement slices may have $<50$ examples. | Medium | **MITIGATED (Phase 9–10)** | Report exact subgroup sample counts ($n$, sarcastic/non-sarcastic split) and 95% bootstrap confidence intervals; explicitly flag any subset with $n < 50$ as exploratory/descriptive per Section 13.5 of the directive. |
| **R-08** | **CPU-Only Compute Constraint** | No discrete NVIDIA CUDA GPU is available (`torch.cuda.is_available() == False`). | Medium | **MITIGATED (Phase 0)** | All neural forward passes (`roberta-base`, `twitter-roberta-base-sentiment`, `wav2vec2-base`) are batched and cached to disk (`data/features/*.parquet` / `.npy`) once, enabling rapid, deterministic multi-seed cross-validation and 10,000-iteration bootstrap testing on CPU. |
